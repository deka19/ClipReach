Transparent Earnings.
Reliable Payouts.

No complex math or hidden fees. Just a straightforward system that rewards authentic reach. Here is exactly how your views turn into cash in your bank account.

01

Submit Link

After editing and publishing your video on TikTok, YouTube Shorts, or Reels, paste the public link into your dashboard. Our system immediately logs your submission.

02

View Audit

Within 48 hours, our team and automated APIs audit your video. We verify the view count, check for required watermarks, and filter out any artificial or botted traffic.

03

Balance Updated

Once verified, your dashboard is updated. The views are multiplied by the campaign's rate (e.g., $45 per 100k views), and the exact dollar amount is added to your Pending Balance.

04

Weekly Cash Out

Hit the $50 minimum threshold and request a withdrawal. We process payments every Friday directly to your preferred payout method with zero platform fees.

Supported Payout Methods

PayPal

Global payouts available in over 150 countries. Standard PayPal conversion rates apply.

UPI / Bank Transfer

Instant, fee-free bank transfers for creators located in India and supported regions.

Crypto (Coming Soon)

USDT and USDC stablecoin payouts are currently in beta testing.

Platform Rules & Requirements

  • The $50 Threshold

    You must accumulate a minimum of $50 in verified earnings before you can request your first withdrawal. This keeps transaction fees manageable for everyone.

  • Strict Anti-Bot Policy

    Any account caught using view bots, engagement pods, or paid traffic will be permanently banned and forfeit all pending earnings. We only pay for real human attention.

  • Payment Schedule

    Withdrawal requests submitted between Monday and Thursday are processed on Friday. Requests submitted on Friday or the weekend are processed the following week.
